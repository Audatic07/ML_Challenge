# V6-TRAIN: a survivor-trained model behind the v5.1 cascade

Session `claude-20260927-1420`, 27 September 2026, from 14:20 IST. Branch
`codex/v6-train-claude-20260927-1420`, based on `db4056b` (stage-2 branch). Every score below is
on the fixed 100k tune partition of the v5.1 plan. The audit partition was not opened for any
choice made here.

## Short version

v6 is a LightGBM pair model trained only on the candidates that survive the v5.1 stage-1 cut:
the top 15 per S1 by v5.1 probability. At test time the v5.1 model keeps the top 12 per S1
with p >= 0.001, and v6 scores exactly those survivors. They form `candidate_pairs.tsv`.

On tune, v6 scores macro F0.5 0.97026 at 6.67 candidates per S1. v5.1 scores 0.96794 on the
same survivors, and the released package scores 0.9605 at 30. The paired per-S1 gain over v5.1
is +0.00232 with a standard error of 0.00019. Both countries and the singletons gain.

## Design

- Stage 1 is the released v5.1 model (`eec93e35...`), unchanged. It scores all retrieved
  candidates (about 256 per S1) in the existing feature shards of `shared/er-v51-20260927/feat/`.
  Nothing in retrieval or features was rebuilt.
- Survivors: sort each S1's candidates by v5.1 p, break ties by target row, keep the top n,
  drop those below the floor. The rule is the same as `v6_stage2.survivors`, and a unit test
  checks that the two agree on tie-heavy input.
- v6 training rows are the fit partition's survivors (top 15). v5.1 was trained on those fit
  rows, so its p is used only to choose survivors and never as a v6 feature. Stop (early
  stopping) and tune rows are out of sample for both models. Fit targets owned by held-out S1
  are removed as in v5.1.
- Features: the same 92 v5.1 features. LightGBM with learning rate 0.06, 255 leaves,
  min_data_in_leaf 50, feature_fraction 0.8, bagging 0.8, up to 5,000 rounds and early stopping
  after 150 rounds without improvement on the stop survivors.
- Tune evaluation: for each cut, the one-owner-per-target rule, then the threshold is swept
  (0.02 grid, then 0.0025 around the best) separately for v6 and for v5.1 on the same
  survivors. The same sweep on the same partition is used for every row, so the small
  optimistic bias is shared.
