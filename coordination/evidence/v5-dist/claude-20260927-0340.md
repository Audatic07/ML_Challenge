# DIST-V5: distributed retrieval, features and LightGBM on SageMaker

Session claude-20260927-0340 took over the SageMaker training run at the user's direction.

## Finding on the inherited run

`entity-res-v4-20260927-astra` recorded 226,881 S1 rows, but `train_source1.tsv` has
2,206,821 rows (see its run_manifest `query_count`). The launched command therefore used
fit=400k, stop=10k and tune=100k on one 8-vCPU/32 GB Studio app. It needed about 2.2 h
for retrieval alone and was expected to run out of memory at the feature/LightGBM stage.
It was left under its 6-hour watchdog.

## Execution

Code: branch `codex/v5-dist-claude-20260927` (`src/v5_dist.py`, `src/v5_vec.py`,
`src/text_norm_v5.py`, `deploy/`). Eight SageMaker notebook instances (the account
limit) run queue workers. Tasks live in the private bucket under `shared/er-v5-*`:
feat shards of 20k queries, train, score and final. Claims use S3 conditional writes.

Measured on real data:

* India catalog, 4.13M targets: normalization plus four channels takes about 120-200 s.
* Unblocked sparse search of 20k India queries takes about 280 s; US takes 400-520 s.
* Features for 2.5M pairs take about 20 s.
* Splitting targets into 250k-column blocks makes `sp_matmul_topn` 10x faster
  (local benchmark, identical top-k up to exact ties). Deployed with hot reload.

## Retrieval benchmark (candidate oracle U)

4,000 tune queries per country, full train catalog, v5.1 normalization, k per source:

| policy | India U | US U | pairs/query (India) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| v5 channels (name/address char and fold @20) | 0.9705 | 0.9926 | 125 |
| + combined name+address char @40 | 0.9789 | 0.9981 | 177 |
| name@20, address@40, folds@10, combo@40, combo_word@20 | 0.9854 | 0.9986 | 194 |
| all channels at k 40-60 | 0.9876 | 0.9989 | 309 |

The v5 baseline shard `feat-train-India-000` (v4 normalization) measured U=0.9725 with
link recall 0.932. Names alone retrieve poorly because many different businesses share
similar names. The combined name+address channels fix most of the misses.
